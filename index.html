Outside work

I grow underwater ecosystems

Aquascaping is landscape design executed entirely underwater. You're balancing biology, chemistry, fluid dynamics, and aesthetics simultaneously — with zero ability to pause or undo. It genuinely teaches systems thinking in a way most hobbies don't.

Every planted tank is a living product. You define the initial conditions, set constraints, and watch emergence happen. The ecosystem tells you when something's wrong before you've noticed it yourself. That feedback loop maps onto platform work more directly than it has any right to.

I run high-tech CO2-injected planted tanks with a particular obsession with Iwagumi layouts. One rock out of place ruins the entire composition — a useful reminder that precision and restraint matter as much as ambition.
